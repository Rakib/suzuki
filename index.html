Tuning notes

Tune the A string first, then D, G and E. Tap a string to hear a steady note, and tap it again to stop. Turn the peg or fine tuner slowly until your string matches and stops wobbling.

Octave practice from Book 2's ear training

Play the open string, then the same note an octave higher with your 3rd finger on the next string up. When the 3rd finger is exactly in tune, the open string starts ringing by itself.

G open G, 3rd finger G on D
D open D, 3rd finger D on A
A open A, 3rd finger A on E

Code reference
  • Pitch + octave + length: A4q C#5h F#5e. Open A is A4; open E is E5.
  • Lengths: w whole, h half, q quarter, e eighth, s sixteenth, t triplet eighth. Add d for dotted: B4dq.
  • A note without # or b is natural, whatever the key: C5q is C natural, Bb4q is B flat.
  • | bar line · Rq rest · , bow lift (next note starts down bow).
  • Repeats: |: and :|. FINE after a note, DC at the end.
  • Endings: [1 … :| [2 … ]. Key change: K:D before the first note in the new key.
  • Show a different finger number with /: Bb5e/4.
  • Force a string with @: E5q@A is E with the 4th finger on the A string.
  • Bows alternate by themselves. Force one with :D or :U: E5q:U.
  • Slur notes into one bow with brackets: (A4e B4e).
  • Tie two notes of the same pitch into one long note with ~ on the first: B4h~ B4q.
  • Marks go at the end of a note: . staccato, > accent, - tenuto, ^ trill. A4e. G4q> D5q-. F#5q^
